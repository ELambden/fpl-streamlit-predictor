What It Does

The app turns live FPL data into a fast workbench for player discovery, public team lookup, transfer advice, and five-gameweek planning.

01

Find transfer targets quickly

Scan current players by club, position, price, minutes, ownership, projected points, fixture run, and risk. The home view is intentionally light so it opens quickly and gets straight to the shortlist.

02

Import a public FPL team

Enter a public team ID to load the squad, captain, vice-captain, bench order, bank context, and immediate transfer options. Team IDs are looked up at runtime through Streamlit cache and are not persisted.

03

Inspect player evidence

Player Lab opens the current-season gameweek table, points and minutes trends, xGI, ICT, ownership movement, and the upcoming forecast line for a selected player.

04

Plan five gameweeks ahead

The planner evaluates hold and transfer paths across the next five gameweeks while respecting FPL squad shape, club limits, budget, free transfers, and hit costs.

Data boundary

Current 2026-27 public FPL data is the authority. Prior 2025-26 player and team information is used only where stable IDs match, and unmatched players fall back to neutral priors until current-season evidence builds up.
