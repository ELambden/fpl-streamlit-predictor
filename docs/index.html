Metric glossary

What the app’s scores mean

predicted_next_gw

Projected FPL points for the next gameweek.

predicted_next5

Projected points across the next five gameweeks.

transfer_score

A target ranking that combines projection, fixture run, differential upside, and risk.

risk_score

A caution score from minutes, starts, and availability. Lower is safer.

fixture_ease

Official FPL difficulty flipped so higher means easier.

optimizer_value

Five-gameweek projection divided by current FPL price.
