Metric Glossary

The app uses a small set of decision metrics. They are designed for comparison, not certainty.

predicted_next_gw

Projected FPL points for the next gameweek, adjusted for availability, recent role, and fixture context.

predicted_next5

Projected points across the next five gameweeks, summing every scheduled fixture in that window.

transfer_score

A ranking score for transfer targets: projected points plus fixture and value upside, less minutes and availability risk.

risk_score

A caution score driven by minutes, start likelihood, injury status, and missing recent evidence. Lower is safer.

fixture_ease

Official FPL fixture difficulty flipped so larger numbers mean an easier upcoming run.

optimizer_value

Projected five-gameweek points per million of current FPL price.
